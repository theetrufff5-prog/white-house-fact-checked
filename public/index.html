Latest receipt

Capital One account closures

Court filing / reporting

Capital One says Trump-affiliated accounts were closed after an anti-money-laundering review

PBS NewsHour reported that Capital One said it closed hundreds of Trump-affiliated bank accounts in 2021 after a months-long review by its anti-money-laundering team. The Trump Organization has argued the closures were politically motivated; Capital One has disputed that characterization.

The key distinction: the bank did not say Donald Trump or the Trump Organization committed money laundering. Concerns raised during an anti-money-laundering review are not the same thing as a finding of criminal conduct.

Documented: Capital One described an anti-money-laundering review and subsequent account closures.

The standard

Claims. Receipts. Reality.

01 / CLAIMS

Quote the actual claim

Start with what was really said or alleged—not a stronger or weaker version invented for the argument.

02 / RECEIPTS

Show the source

Use court records, legislation, official data, primary documents and high-quality reporting whenever possible.

03 / REALITY

State the limits

Separate what the evidence proves from what it suggests, disputes, alleges or leaves unanswered.
