Capital One says Trump-affiliated accounts were closed after anti-money-laundering review

PBS NewsHour reports that Capital One said it closed hundreds of Trump-affiliated bank accounts in 2021 after a months-long review by its anti-money-laundering team. The Trump Organization has argued the closures were politically motivated; Capital One has denied that characterization.

The key distinction: Capital One did not accuse Donald Trump or the Trump Organization of money laundering. The bank said activity raised concerns under its anti-money-laundering review process and that the account decisions followed its policies and banking-compliance obligations.

What to remember

Documented: Capital One described an anti-money-laundering review and account closures.

Not established: The bank did not say Trump or his organization committed money laundering.
